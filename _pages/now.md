# Now

## Where

In Pollica, on the Cilento coast of Italy, through mid-July for the [Save the Ocean bootcamp](https://climateshapers.org/en/bootcamp-save-the-ocean/), then back to the Pacific Northwest. Boats, mountains, and a laptop.

## Work

- **[Astraeus Ocean Systems](https://astraeusocean.com/)**: co-founding. Building the Ocean Intelligence Platform: sensing infrastructure that turns raw ocean data into decisions for climate, conservation, and industry. The phase-one coastal-monitoring build is now pointed at Pollica.
- **REAP Climate Center**: acting Director of Grant Strategy, leading multi-million-dollar grant initiatives and standing up a workforce development program for bio-regenerative and climate-adjacent careers.
- **[Island Lab](https://islandlab.dev)** (2026–): coordinating tall ships and living, learning labs in the San Juans and the Strait of Juan de Fuca, pulling artists, craftspeople, and policymakers around the ocean. Currently learning the tall ship *[Adventuress](https://www.soundexp.org/)* out of Port Townsend, with a [Salish voyage in September](https://islandlab.dev/september-2026/salish-voyage.html).

## Making

- Environmental engagement systems in Pollica with the Future Food Institute community: grassroots collaboration to help a culturally rich, isolated region cultivate its own ecosystem and economy on its own terms.
- The next phase of this site: calendar log, tag graph, hand-built CSS (see [VISION](https://github.com/skolk/skolk.github.io/blob/master/_backend/VISION.md) and [the log](/log)).

- Personal infrastructure: how to keep a site that's a 10-year ledger, not a 10-week portfolio.
