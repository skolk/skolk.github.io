# Sulfi

<img src="/images/blog_posts/SVP/IMG_7236.jpg" width="100%" alt="Sulfi, a yellow Portland Pudgy dinghy, pulled up on a gravel river bank in Southeast Alaska, forested mountains and a snow-streaked peak behind">

*Sulfi* is [Petrichor]({{ '/petrichor' | prepend: site.baseurl }})'s dinghy, a yellow [Portland Pudgy](https://www.portlandpudgy.com/). She is the small boat that makes the big boat useful: the way we get from the anchor to the beach, run a stern line ashore, or go poke into a river mouth the mothership cannot reach.

## What she is

The Portland Pudgy is a rigid, unsinkable rowing and sailing dinghy, built as a hard-shell hull with foam-filled buoyancy rather than an inflatable tube. That is the whole reason we like her. She rows well, she takes a beating on rocks and gravel without going flat, and she doubles as a proper safety craft. For a boat that spends its summers in cold, remote water, a dinghy you can trust is not a luxury.

## Where she goes

*Sulfi* has beached on gravel bars in Southeast Alaska and sat on the ramp in San Francisco Bay. Wherever *Petrichor* anchors, *Sulfi* is what closes the last hundred yards to shore.
