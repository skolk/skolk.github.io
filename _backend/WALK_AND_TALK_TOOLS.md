# Walk-and-talk: the tools

The morning practice for the small tools. One prompt per tool, ten minutes of voice-to-text on the walk to 9zero, paste the transcript back, and the `/morning` flow turns it into a draft or a rewrite. Each prompt asks the same four things, because the write-ups that work (Seventy-Eight Cards, Wrong Without an Error) all answer them: what was bugging you, what the tool does about it, the one design decision that surprised you, and what you would build next. The last one is the point of the practice: the write-up is where the next project gets picked.

Order, decided 2026-09-06: the four drafts already on the page come first (items 3 and 4 here, plus the July and August recaps), one per morning. Then the two tools with a manual but no story, netmeter and micmeter. Check one off when the post ships.

---

## 1. netmeter, the post
**Target:** a `type: project` post that points at `/projects/netmeter/` (the page is the manual; the post is the story)

The page is exhaustive and nobody will read it top to bottom. Tell the story instead.
- The morning half a gigabyte was gone by 9am. What you tried first, and why every existing tool told you one piece.
- The nettop counter that looked cumulative and was not. Chrome "moving" 1.65 GB in thirteen minutes.
- August: the day the problem stopped being counting and became bookkeeping. WiFi Signal frozen for four days.
- Would you build it again, or buy TripMode? What does the hotspot cost now versus June?
